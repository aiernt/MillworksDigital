What I build

Two kinds of work: the things your customers see, and the tools your team uses behind the scenes.

For your customers

Websites and stores that represent your business well and are easy to use.

  • Business websites

    Designed and built from scratch around your business.

  • Online stores

    Storefronts and product catalogs that make browsing and buying simple.

  • Sites you can update

    Your staff can change text, photos, and pages without calling me.

  • Redesigns and speed fixes

    New life for an existing site that looks dated or loads slowly.

For your team

Custom software for the work that happens behind the counter.

  • Dashboards and business tools

    Internal apps that put your numbers and daily tasks in one place.

  • Workflow automation

    The repetitive steps in your process, handled automatically.

  • Integrations

    Connect the software you already use, so information moves between systems without retyping.

  • Maintenance and support

    Fixes, updates, and new features after launch, from the person who built it.

How a project goes

Four steps, and you're involved in each one.

  1. 1

    Talk it through

    We start with a conversation about your business, your goals, and who the site or tool needs to work for. Design starts once that's clear.

  2. 2

    Plan it together

    I map out the structure, content, and look, and we review it together, so nothing is a surprise once building starts.

  3. 3

    Build in the open

    You see progress as it happens, with regular check-ins along the way.

  4. 4

    Launch and keep going

    Your site or tool goes live, and I stay on to fix, extend, and support it as your business grows.

Why a millstone

Fort Mill grew up around a grist mill on Steele Creek and later became a textile-mill town. It was built by people who made things that worked, and kept working, for a long time.

A millstone does one job, every day, for years. It takes rough grain and turns it into something people can use. Millworks Digital holds its websites and software to that same standard, and every project is built carefully by one person you can actually reach.
