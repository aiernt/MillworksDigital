Why a millstone

Fort Mill grew up around a grist mill on Steele Creek and later became a textile-mill town. It was built by people who made things that worked, and kept working, for a long time. The stone on this page is modeled on a millstone from Webb's Mill that's on display in town today.

A millstone does one job, every day, for years. It takes rough grain and turns it into something people can use. Millworks Digital holds its websites and software to that same standard, and every project is built carefully right here in Fort Mill, by someone you can sit down with.

Common questions

The things most people ask before getting started.

Who owns my website?

You do. The site and the domain are yours from day one. If you ever stop the Millwright Plan, everything is handed over so you can host it anywhere.

How long does it take?

A Starter site is usually live in about two weeks. Business and Custom projects depend on their size, and you'll get a timeline along with your quote.

What do I need to have ready?

Your logo, a few photos, and the basics about your business. If your photos or wording aren't ready yet, that's fine. We'll sort it out together during planning.

How does payment work?

Half up front to get started, and the rest when your site goes live. The Millwright Plan is billed monthly after launch.

Do I have to sign up for the Millwright Plan?

No, it's optional. Choosing a 12-month plan saves 20% on your build, up to $500. Without a plan, you can host the site yourself and pay for changes as you need them.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. Redesigns, speed improvements, and accessibility fixes for existing sites are all part of the work, and so is support for a site someone else built.

Do you only work with Fort Mill businesses?

Not at all. Millworks is based in Fort Mill and works with businesses across the Carolinas. Around Charlotte and Rock Hill we can meet in person, and anywhere else works just as well by phone and video.
